Grasp OS is an internal operating system for AI at work. It gives your organization a single place for company knowledge, Apps with React screens and durable workflows, an AI agent, and the governance controls — permissions, audit log, model gateway — that make running AI in a business safe and auditable. Every deployment runs in your own Cloudflare account, in the EU jurisdiction, with your data never leaving it.
